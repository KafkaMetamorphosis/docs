# Franz

Status: **draft**

Franz is the **control plane** for an async fleet. It stores the declared state of
async resources, applies fleet-wide governance, and receives signals about their
realization. Franz never connects to Kafka — agents do the real-world work
(see `../001-architecture-overview.md`, `../004-kafka-topic-reconciliation/`, `../005-odradek/`).

## Authoritative contract

The wire contract lives in **`../../api/proto/franz/v1/`**. These documents describe
intent, invariants, and behaviour; the `.proto` files define the messages, gRPC
services, and their REST/JSON gateway mapping. Where the two differ, the `.proto`
is authoritative for shapes and the docs are authoritative for semantics.

## Removed: the expansion engine

Async Channel expansion is no longer modelled as a standalone engine. Placement is a
step of channel create/update (`003.4`): match the channel's context selector to
eligible clusters, honour `franz.affinity/shard-size` and taints, and create the
Kafka Topics and their first Topic Revisions in the same transaction. The
`franz.logic.expansion` / `franz.ops.expansion` split, the async re-expansion queue,
and the stale-revision guard are dropped; reconciliation guarantees come from the
Topic Revision model in `003.3`.
