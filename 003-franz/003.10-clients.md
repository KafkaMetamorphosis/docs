# Clients

Status: **ready**

Shapes & RPCs: [`client.proto`](../../franz/api/franz/v1/client.proto) — `Client`,
`ClientChannelAccess`, `ObservedConsumerGroup`, `ClientService`.

## Concepts

A **Client** is the identity a service authenticates as when it reads from or
writes to Async Channels through the Franz SDK. It is **fleet-wide**: one flat
namespace across the whole realm, not per-channel or per-team.

A Client **carries no permission of its own** — no Read/Write role, no channel
list. Every access decision is made by the target channel's **access policy**
(`003.5`), which matches the client by FRN or by its labels.

## Key fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `name` | Client-provided, **realm-wide unique**, immutable. Also the default consumer-group prefix. |
| `frn` | Server-assigned (`frn:<realm>:client:<name>`). |
| `labels` | Free-form metadata; **should** carry an owner label (`org.com/owner=…`). Channel access policies match against these. Enforcement of the owner label is an open question. |
| `created_at` / `updated_at` | Audit pair. |

There is **no** type, role, or state field.

## Consumer groups

Consumer groups are **not registered in Franz**.

- **Default name** — `<client-name>.<topic-name>` (one group per shard the client
  consumes).
- **Custom names** are allowed; a Telemetry Agent flags them with
  `ObservedConsumerGroup.custom = true`.
- Franz learns of groups only from **Telemetry Agents** (`003.9`), which report
  `ObservedConsumerGroup { group, async_channel, kafka_topic, custom,
  reported_by_agent, last_seen_at }` via `telemetry.proto`. This is observation,
  not declaration.
- A default-named group is attributed to its client by the name prefix.
  Attribution of a **custom-named** group to a client is an open question.

## Derived views

- **`ListClientChannelAccess`** — every Async Channel whose access policy matches
  this client, with the `effective` permissions and `matched_by`. The reverse of
  `AsyncChannelService.ListChannelClients` (`003.5`).
- **`ListObservedConsumerGroups`** — the telemetry-observed groups for this
  client.

Both are computed on read; neither is stored declared state.

## State transitions

A Client has no lifecycle state. `CreateClient` → label edits via `UpdateClient` →
`DeleteClient`. Deleting a client does **not** free its `name` / FRN (consistent
with other entities); FRN-matched access-policy statements go dormant (`003.5`).

## Invariants

- `name` is realm-wide unique and immutable.
- A Client never holds a permission; `003.5` is the sole authority for Read/Write.
- `CreateClient` / `UpdateClient` carry only `name` and `labels`.
- `ClientChannelAccess` and `ObservedConsumerGroup` are read-only projections —
  never written through `ClientService`.

## Cross-entity behavior

- **Access Policy** (`003.5`) — a `Principal` matches a client by `client_frn`
  (with `*`) or by a label selector over the client's `labels`.
- **Async Channel** (`003.4`) — the client connects to a channel via the SDK; a
  paused channel makes the data path unavailable but leaves the client unchanged.
- **Kafka Topic** (`003.6`) — an observed group names the specific shard
  (`kafka_topic`) it consumes.
- **Telemetry Agent** (`003.9`) — the only source of `ObservedConsumerGroup`
  records; `reported_by_agent` names it.

## Deferred to a later ADR

- **Client credentials** — token / key issuance, rotation, and how a connecting
  client proves it is this `Client`.
- **Connection testing** — a "can this client reach that channel" probe.

## Open questions

1. **Owner label** — is `org.com/owner` required at `CreateClient`, or only
   conventional?
2. **Custom consumer-group attribution** — how a non-default group name is tied
   back to a client (declared at connect, a label, or left unattributed).
3. **Deletion semantics** — is the `name` ever reclaimable; what happens to the
   client's `ObservedConsumerGroup` records and to dormant policy statements
   (cross-ref `003.5` / `003.11`).
4. **`ObservedConsumerGroup` retention** — how long a group with a stale
   `last_seen_at` is kept before Franz drops it.
5. **Scope** — Client is realm-wide today; whether it ever needs a
   team / namespace sub-scope.
