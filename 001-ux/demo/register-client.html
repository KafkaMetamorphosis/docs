Register Client

Record an identity that will connect to Async Channels. Permissions are granted later by each channel's access policy.

Client details

A client has a name and labels. It has no Read or Write role — that comes from the channels it is granted access to.

Identity

The client authenticates with this identity through the Franz SDK.

ORN: orn:acme-platform:client:<name>

Labels

Labels identify the client — its owner, team, environment — and are what channel access policies match against.

Add at least an org.com/owner label.

Consumer groups

Consumer groups are not registered here. When the client subscribes to a channel it uses the group <client-name>.<topic> by default, or a custom group it passes to the SDK. Telemetry Agents report the groups they observe and link each one back to this client and its owner.

What happens next?

Franz records the client in the control plane. The client gets no access until a channel's access policy grants it, matched on this client's ORN or labels. Client credentials and connection testing are outside this flow.
